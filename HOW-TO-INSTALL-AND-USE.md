# WhatsApp Outreach — How to Install & Use

A simple desktop app for sending personalized, one-by-one WhatsApp messages
from your own WhatsApp account. You stay in control: the app prepares each
message, and **you** click Send.

---

## Before you start — one requirement

You must have **Google Chrome** installed on your computer.
If you don't have it: download it free from https://www.google.com/chrome

(That's the only thing you need. No other setup.)

---

## Step 1 — Install the app

1. Double-click **`WhatsApp Outreach Setup 1.0.0.exe`** (the file you were sent).
2. Windows may show a blue box that says **"Windows protected your PC."**
   This is normal for in-house apps. Click **More info**, then click
   **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer (click Next / Install). When it finishes, a
   **WhatsApp Outreach** icon appears on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

You only install once.

---

## Step 2 — Open the app

Double-click the **WhatsApp Outreach** icon on your Desktop.

---

## Step 3 — Set up your campaign

The app has three steps along the top: **1 Setup → 2 Message → 3 Media**.

**1 Setup**
- Click **Upload CSV** and choose your contacts file.
- Your CSV needs two columns: **phone** and **name**.
- Phone numbers must include the country code, e.g. `919168833977`
  (no spaces, no "+", no dashes).

   Example CSV:
   ```
   phone,name
   919168833977,Rutvij
   919812345678,Anita
   ```

**2 Message**
- Type your message. Use `{{name}}` to insert each person's name.
- Example: `Hi {{name}}, inviting you to our event!`
- The preview shows how it will look.

**3 Media (optional)**
- Add an image and/or video if you want.
- The text goes first, then the image/video as a separate message.

---

## Step 4 — Run it

1. Click **Start Campaign** (it's at the bottom-left, visible on every step).
2. A Chrome window opens showing **WhatsApp Web**.
3. **The first time only:** scan the QR code with your phone
   (WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device).
4. For each contact, the app opens the chat and fills in the message.
   When it says **"ready"**, click the green **Send** button (➤) in WhatsApp,
   then click **Next Contact** in the app.
5. Repeat until done. The app shows progress (Sent / Remaining) at the top.

---

## Tips

- **Send to yourself first** as a test before a real campaign.
- Keep batches reasonable and only message people who expect to hear from you.
- You can **Pause**, **Skip**, or **Stop** at any time using the buttons.
- Closing the app stops everything.

---

## Important

This tool is for **consent-based, human-assisted outreach** only. You manually
send every message. Please use it responsibly and in line with WhatsApp's
Terms of Service.

---

## Trouble?

- **"Chromium not found" / browser won't open** → install Google Chrome.
- **Nothing happens after Start** → make sure you scanned the QR code.
- **Numbers show as not registered** → check the phone has the country code
  and no spaces or symbols.
